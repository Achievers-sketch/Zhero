1 INTRODUCTION

This placeholder describes a fictional ultrasound unit with four subsystems: power,
cooling, probe, and display and control. It exists so that retrieval, citation and
verification features can be tested against known text.

1.1 Intended audience

The user-level sections are written for operators. Sections marked SERVICE LEVEL are
written for qualified biomedical engineering staff only.

2 DAILY OPERATION

2.1 Power-on check

1. Connect the unit to a suitable mains supply.
2. Switch on the unit and wait for the start-up screen.
3. Confirm that no error banner is shown before scanning begins.

WARNING: Do not use the unit if the power cable or plug is visibly damaged. Report the
damage to qualified service staff.

2.2 Cooling and ventilation

The fictional ZH-DEMO unit uses an internal cooling fan. In normal operation the fan runs
at a steady speed and the internal temperature stays within the demonstration range.
Keep the ventilation openings at the rear and sides of the unit clear of obstructions.

If a fan speed warning or high temperature warning is displayed, stop scanning, allow the
unit to rest, check that the ventilation openings are not blocked, and contact qualified
service staff if the warning persists.

CAUTION: Never operate the unit with the cooling openings covered.

2.3 Probe handling

Inspect the probe cable and connector before each use. Do not use a probe with a cracked
housing or a damaged cable. Clean the probe according to your facility's approved
procedure after each patient.

3 TROUBLESHOOTING FOR OPERATORS

3.1 Fan speed warning

A fan speed warning means the cooling fan is running slower than expected. Possible
documented causes in this fictional manual are blocked ventilation openings, dust
build-up, or a failing fan. Operators may check the openings; any internal inspection is
SERVICE LEVEL and must be performed only by qualified staff.

3.2 Unstable power indication

An unstable power indication means the supply voltage is fluctuating. Check that the
plug is fully inserted and that the unit is not sharing a circuit with heavy equipment.
Do not open the unit. Contact qualified service staff if the indication continues.

3.3 Probe not detected

If the probe is not detected, power the unit down, reseat the probe connector, and power
the unit on again. If the probe is still not detected, try a different probe port and
contact qualified service staff.

4 SERVICE LEVEL INFORMATION

WARNING: This section is for qualified biomedical engineering staff only. Disconnect the
unit from the mains supply before any internal work.

4.1 Cooling subsystem inspection

Qualified staff should inspect the fan, the air path and the temperature sensor. Part
number FAN-2040-X is the fictional replacement fan for the ZH-DEMO unit. Record the fan
speed and internal temperature before and after any maintenance.

4.2 Power subsystem inspection

Qualified staff should measure the input supply and inspect the power connector and
internal power module. Part number PSU-310-A is the fictional replacement power module.

4.3 Preventive maintenance schedule

The fictional schedule is: clean ventilation openings monthly, inspect cables quarterly,
and perform a documented performance check annually. Follow your facility's own policy
where it differs.
